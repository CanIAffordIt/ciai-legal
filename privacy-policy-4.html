Privacy Policy

Can I Afford It? · Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy explains how the Can I Afford It? iPhone app ("the app", "we") handles your information. The app is provided by the Can I Afford It? team. Contact: support@ciaiapp.info.

The short version

Your finances, saved results, and goals stay on your iPhone. Numbers leave your device only when you choose to ask the AI assistant about a result, your budget, or a savings goal, or when you choose to connect a bank, as described below. The app has no advertising or analytics, and we do not sell your information.

Information you enter

To estimate affordability, the app asks for details you choose to provide: income, monthly expenses by category, available cash, details of items you are considering (such as price, down payment, loan term, and interest rate), savings goals, your budget, your budget style quiz answer, and results you save. This information is stored on your device. It is transmitted only when you use the AI assistant (see below).

How it is protected

Your data is saved in the app's private storage using iOS Data Protection, which encrypts it while your device is locked. It is excluded from iCloud and computer backups. The app hides its contents in the app switcher.

Information collected automatically

The app does not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, or usage analytics, and it contains no third-party advertising or tracking code. If you install a test version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports and any feedback you choose to send, under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

We use Supabase to host our server and to give each installation an anonymous sign-in ID (no name or email). We use Anthropic, the maker of Claude, to generate AI answers. If you choose to connect a bank, we use Plaid to link it (see "Bank connection" below).

AI assistant

If you tap "Ask AI" and agree to the notice, the app sends your question plus numbers to our server. For a result: item category, price and loan terms (for investments, the type and time horizon you chose), your monthly income, your total monthly expenses, your cash on hand, and the calculated results. For your budget: your budget style, monthly income, expense amounts by category, the categories and monthly cost of purchases and goals in your budget, cash on hand, and calculated totals. For a goal: its category, target, amount saved, monthly savings, and your income, expenses, and cash. Names you type for goals and purchases are not sent. It does not send your name, email, or account numbers. Our server passes this to Anthropic to write a reply. We do not store your questions or numbers; we keep only a daily count of questions per anonymous ID to prevent abuse. Anthropic processes the information under its own privacy policy. AI answers can be wrong and are not financial advice. Conversations for saved results and goals are kept on your device so you can read them again; they are never stored on our server, and you can clear a chat or erase everything at any time.

Bank connection (optional)

Connecting a bank is optional. If you tap "Connect My Bank", Plaid's screen opens and you sign in to your bank with Plaid. Your bank login goes to Plaid and is never seen or stored by us. Plaid's handling of your information is described in Plaid's privacy policy. We store a Plaid access token for your connection on our server (with Supabase) so the app can read your recurring bills and the last 30 days of spending. From that, the server sends your device only totals and the names of recurring bills, which you review before anything is used. We do not store your transactions or account numbers, we do not sell this data, and the AI assistant never receives bank names or account numbers. You can disconnect at any time under Settings → Disconnect Bank, which removes the connection and deletes the token from our server.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can erase everything at any time in the app under Settings → Delete All My Data, or by deleting the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because we do not receive or store your information, we have no personal data about you to access, correct, or delete. If you have a privacy question or request, contact us at the address above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do, and we will give notice in the app for material changes.
